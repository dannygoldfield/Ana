# Competitive Landscape Notes

The current landscape suggests that many pieces of Ana already exist separately:

- offline AI tutors
- educational software for low-connectivity regions
- screen-free child companions
- shared offline learning hubs
- local-language educational AI
- solar-powered educational infrastructure

The current Ana hypothesis is differentiated by the combination of:

1. Girls as the primary user.
2. A device that personally belongs to one girl.
3. Full normal operation without internet.
4. Privacy designed into computation and physical interaction.
5. An educational philosophy centered on agency and independent thought.

## Strategic warning

"Offline AI on a device" is unlikely to be a durable moat. Model capability and local inference will become increasingly common.

Potential defensibility is more likely to come from:

- trusted educational leadership
- co-design with girls
- field research
- regional language and curriculum expertise
- safety
- industrial design
- distribution relationships
- evidence of educational outcomes
- trust
- mission governance

## Main competitive question

A shared classroom or household hub could be cheaper per learner than personal hardware.

Ana therefore needs evidence that personal ownership, privacy, continuity, and freedom from household device allocation create enough additional educational value to justify one device per girl.
