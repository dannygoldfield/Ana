# Vision

## The problem

In some households where capable technology is scarce, the best device may remain with adults. Children may receive older or less capable devices, and girls may have the least access or none at all.

If confirmed through field research, this is another obstacle to education.

Ana begins with a different assumption from most educational technology:

> A girl may not control access to the household's best device.

## The opportunity

Give one girl one private, capable, personally owned thinking companion.

Ana should work without the internet, without a cloud account, without advertising, and without depending on a family phone.

## Ownership of thought

Ana is designed around ownership of thought.

The device belongs to one person. Her questions, ideas, notes, and learning remain local unless she deliberately exports them.

Ana should help her think rather than train her to defer to technology.

## Mission

How do we put a trustworthy thinking companion into the hands of every girl, regardless of whether she has internet access?

## Philosophy

**Ask. Nurture. Aspire.**

- **Ask**: curiosity begins with questions.
- **Nurture**: support understanding, confidence, and independent thought.
- **Aspire**: help each girl imagine and pursue a larger future.
