# Ideas and Open Questions

This file is intentionally informal.

## Product

- Permanently attached single earbud
- Cord wraps into a channel or recess in the enclosure
- Earbud snaps securely into the body when stored
- Body microphone vs inline microphone should be tested
- One-hand, one-ear use
- Voice only for the earliest prototype
- Small e-ink display only if proven useful
- Ana Solar
- Physical offline update mechanism
- Regional language packs
- Region-specific knowledge libraries

## Educational behavior

- Ask questions rather than always answer immediately
- Admit uncertainty
- Distinguish fact from interpretation
- Encourage verification of important claims
- Encourage the user to form her own view
- Avoid becoming an authority figure or emotional dependency
- Measure learning, confidence, curiosity, and agency rather than engagement alone

## Research

- How common is unequal household device allocation by gender?
- Is the effect educationally meaningful?
- Does personal ownership improve use and learning compared with shared devices?
- What ages benefit most?
- What does privacy mean to girls themselves?
- Would families permit girls to personally control such a device?
- What would cause fathers, brothers, or other adults to appropriate it?
- What physical and social design choices protect the girl's ownership?
- Which languages and regions should a first pilot serve?
- Which educational needs matter more than the ones we currently imagine?

## Business / governance

- Mission-driven nonprofit
- Public-benefit company
- Foundation plus commercial licensee
- Commercial versions whose revenue subsidizes mission deployments
- Open-source components with protected brand, curriculum, or safety layer
- Avoid deciding structure until there is a validated problem, team, and prototype
